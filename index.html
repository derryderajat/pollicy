Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

1. Introduction

DerryStudio ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Instagram automation service that responds to comments and direct messages on behalf of our business clients. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you interact with our Instagram automation tools.

2. Information We Collect

Through the Instagram API, we may collect:

  • Public Profile Information: Instagram username, profile picture, and public bio
  • Comments: Text content of comments left on our connected Instagram business accounts
  • Direct Messages: Message content sent to our connected Instagram business accounts
  • Interaction Metadata: Timestamps and message IDs for automation logging

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Automated response to customer comments and inquiries
  • Customer service and support automation
  • Workflow analytics and system improvement

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties. We may share data with:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (as required by Instagram API integration)
  • Our automation platform provider (n8n) for workflow execution

5. Data Retention

We retain interaction data for 30 days for operational purposes, after which it is automatically deleted. Message content is processed in real-time and not stored long-term.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of automated responses by contacting us directly

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, API key protection, and access token rotation to protect your data.

8. Third-Party Services

Our service relies on:

  • Meta Instagram API - subject to Meta's Privacy Policy
  • n8n - workflow automation platform

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
